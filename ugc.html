#why-ugc

People don't hate ads. They hate boring ones.

Traditional ads get skipped in half a second. UGC gets watched to the end — because it doesn't feel like marketing. It feels like a recommendation from a friend.

/01
The first 2 seconds decide everything.

On TikTok, viewers decide whether to keep watching almost instantly. A weak hook kills the video before your product ever appears. I write and test hooks first — the edit serves the hook, not the other way around.

Hook-first, every single video
/02
Authenticity beats production value.

Consumers consistently trust creators more than brands — a phone-shot video of a real person using your product routinely outperforms polished studio ads. I make videos that look and feel like what your audience already chooses to watch.

Native, not polished-to-death
/03
The algorithm rewards watch time.

Every platform ranks content on one core signal: do people keep watching? That's why I edit for retention — pattern interrupts, pacing, loops, and payoffs — so the algorithm has every reason to push your video to the next million screens.

Engineered for retention
/04
Trends have a shelf life of days.

A trend that's hot on Monday is dead by Friday. Working with an agency means catching the wave two weeks late. I move at feed speed — brief to posted-ready video in 48–72 hours, while the trend still has teeth.

Trend-responsive delivery
